Starlight Collection
Starlight Run

Guide a lantern through 30 seconds of night, collecting golden stars and avoiding drifting shadows. A small game about movement, decisions, and immediate feedback.

  • Mouse, touch, WASD, and arrow-key controls
  • Live scoring and a 30-second countdown
  • Particle feedback and locally saved best scores
  • Responsive desktop and mobile layout

MILESTONE 001

From an idea to a playable prototype

Build movement, collection, collisions, and end-of-round feedback first so the game becomes testable early. Add mouse, touch, and keyboard input for different devices.

NEXTIteration

OPTIMIZATION PLAN

Add depth to play, beyond difficulty

Future iterations will explore rare stars and combos for risk and reward, then adjust pacing with staged speed curves. Change one variable at a time and evaluate it through playtesting.

Design principle

Make feedback clear before adding complexity. Extend rules with data objects before considering a structural rewrite.
