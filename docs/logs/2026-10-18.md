### 06.1 — Extend City DNA: 3 → 6

- Added `chicagoGrid` (Grid), `amsterdamCanalRing` (Canal-Street), `lisbonHillCore` (Organic) purely as configuration: `CITY_DNA` entries with conditions / conflicts / hints / `unsupported`, `DNA_NETWORK_OVERRIDES` + `strategyParams()`, parcel and building rules. One small hook: optional `fixedAxisDeg` for Chicago's cardinal grid.
- Result: 72 / 72 runs ok; original 3 DNAs 36 / 36 bit-identical to a baseline recorded before the change; reproducibility 6 / 6; all 13 seed-terrain verifiers pass.
- Differences on the shared terrain (coast 1101), e.g. Chicago: cardinal axis, tight tower core (p90 98.8 m, max 163 m), FAR 1.45 vs Manhattan 4.5; Amsterdam: 6.3 m frontage, uniform 3–5 storeys; Lisbon: 1001 small hillside parcels.
- Low-difference cases explained, not decorated: Amsterdam vs Venice on mountain 1101 (no water → canal rules inactive); Chicago vs Manhattan where the land mask truncates most grid lines.
- Unsupported (recorded in config): Chicago alleys and transit-node height peaks; Amsterdam concentric ring canals; Lisbon stairs / funiculars, Baixa valley grid, viewpoints.
- Chicago and Amsterdam never win auto-match on the 12 test terrains (no landform has a river through a plain or a canal belt).

### 06.2 — World Grid & City Placement

- New entry `/experiments/world-grid/`: 9 fixed 1024 m chunks (`chunk_<gx>_<gy>`, gx, gy ∈ {−1, 0, 1}), each running the unchanged single-chunk chain.
- Seed rule: `terrainSeed = 1 + hash('world-grid-terrain', worldSeed, gridX, gridY, landform) mod 9999` — slot order never enters the hash.
- City ID per slot: empty / auto / any `CITY_DNA` key; changing one City ID rebuilds only that chunk's city layers.
- Save / restore of the world config (re-derived seeds; a tampered seed is rejected).
- Placement decision: landform rules sample absolute coordinates, so each chunk is generated in its local frame and placed by translating the grid origin; the only seed-terrain change was `ChunkView` translating its plane to a non-zero origin.
- Result: placement exact (no overlap, no gap); centre chunk bit-identical to the single-chunk page; 7 / 7 placed chunks match their origin-0 generation; first build ≈ 18–22 s, one City ID change 2.3–3.2 s.
- Seams were only detected (12 / 12 height steps up to 296 m, 6 one-sided water edges) — joined the next day in 06.3a.
