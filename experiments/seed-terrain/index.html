A frozen 1024 m chunk generated by four landform rules: plain (fBm + gentle tilt), river valley (continuous valley cut with a sloped river surface), coast (warped shoreline + sea-level mask), and mountain (ridged multifractal). The edge transition zone is a smooth weight field only; terrain is not flattened. No roads, parcels, or buildings.
