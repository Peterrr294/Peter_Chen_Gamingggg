MULTI-ORIGIN PROCEDURAL URBAN GROWTH

Six cores,
one growing world.

Six City DNAs (Venice, Manhattan, Fez, Amsterdam, Lisbon, Chicago) each start from one core chunk placed by the world seed with a minimum spacing, on the best-fitting terrain among sampled sites. Each step a fair scheduler picks one city, scores its frontier cells on real terrain (DNA fit, buildable low-slope land, road ports toward the cell, water, DNA growth tendency, seeded jitter) and grows one 1024 m chunk next to a parent of the same city. Terrain depends only on the world seed and the cell; chunks are never overwritten. Seams between independently generated chunks are measured and shown, not hidden. All weights are design hypotheses.
