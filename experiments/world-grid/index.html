PROCEDURAL MINI WORLD / 06.2 WORLD GRID

GENERATING

WORLD GRID / 3 × 3 CHUNKS

Nine chunks,
one world frame.

Each slot is a fixed 1024 m chunk at a fixed grid coordinate. Its terrain seed is derived from the world seed, the grid coordinate, and its landform; its City ID (empty, auto, or a City DNA) only drives the downstream city layers. Shared edges are harmonised inside the transition band where a natural slope is possible; edges that cannot be joined keep their raw step and are reported. No city influence is applied yet.
