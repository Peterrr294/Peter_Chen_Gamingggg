      OPEN LEARNING GUIDE

      Where is the shader?

      JavaScript creates geometry and a ShaderMaterial, then Three.js submits both to the GPU. The vertex stage places vertices; rasterization creates fragments between them; the fragment stage chooses their colors.

      How does a fragment know position?

      The vertex shader writes varyings such as vUv and vWorldPosition. The GPU interpolates them across the triangle. Screen position is also available as gl_FragCoord.

      What is shared?

      Attributes can differ per vertex. Uniforms stay constant for one draw call. Varyings begin per vertex and become interpolated values for each fragment.

      What is intentionally absent?

      No lights, textures, noise, FBM, PBR, terrain materials, ray marching, WFC, or post-processing are used in this round.
