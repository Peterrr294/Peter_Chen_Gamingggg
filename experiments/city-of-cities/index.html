A CITY OF CITIES / MULTI-ORIGIN INITIALIZATION

Six origins,
one world frame.

Each of the six City DNAs (Venice, Manhattan, Fez, Amsterdam, Lisbon, Chicago) samples candidate sites from its own city seed, evaluates them on real terrain (slope, buildable land, water, terrain type, DNA fit) and takes a reproducible weighted choice at a minimum distance from the other origins. Each origin is one independent 1024 m core chunk generated by the unchanged single-chunk chain with its own City DNA. Terrain belongs to the world (world seed + cell), not to the city. No growth happens in this stage: the six cores are generation 0. All weights are design hypotheses.
