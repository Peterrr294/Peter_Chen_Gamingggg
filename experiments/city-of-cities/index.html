GENERATING

A CITY OF CITIES / ORIGINS + PROCEDURAL GROWTH

Six origins,
one growing world.

Each of the six City DNAs (Venice, Manhattan, Fez, Amsterdam, Lisbon, Chicago) samples candidate sites from its own city seed, evaluates them on real terrain (slope, buildable land, water, terrain type, DNA fit) and takes a reproducible weighted choice at a minimum distance from the other origins. Each origin is one independent 1024 m core chunk generated by the unchanged single-chunk chain with its own City DNA. Terrain belongs to the world (world seed + cell), not to the city. G02: Grow one step adds at most one chunk, chosen from the cities’ growth frontiers (north, south, east, west of their chunks) by terrain, water, infrastructure and Growth DNA scores, a fair city bid and a seeded weighted choice; each new chunk runs the same chain with its city’s DNA. Seams between chunks are recorded as conflicts, not repaired. All weights are design hypotheses.
